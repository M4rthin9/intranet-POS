# Payment statement and cash settlement

Reports opens with a bank-statement-style payment ledger: event and Thai reporting dates, total receipts, PromptPay and cash summaries, customer/receipt/reference details, and a running total. Search filters the displayed rows; the totals and CSV always cover the full selected period. Print/PDF uses A4 and repeats the table header.

This is an internal payment statement, not an imported bank statement or automatic bank matching. It includes currently confirmed, uncleared payments only. Opening receipts are currently confirmed payments before the selected start date. Closing receipts equal opening receipts plus confirmed payments in the selected period. Voided/refunded payments are excluded, including from opening receipts; use the existing VOID export for correction history. Payment dates use confirmation time in Asia/Bangkok. Cash is separate from PromptPay to support comparing bank credits.

At the payment counter, select PromptPay QR or Cash before preparing the payment. The method is recorded when the payment is prepared and remains fixed; a pending payment must be completed or voided by Super Admin before changing its method. A cash payment does not require PromptPay settings or generate a bank QR. Cash confirmation requires enough money to cover the outstanding amount and the cashier's explicit verification. The cash receipt includes the net sale total, amount received and change. Cash references are generated by the server. Confirming a payment settles the same orders and customer account and creates the same immutable, audited receipt as the existing PromptPay flow.

No database schema migration is needed. Existing PromptPay payments, receipt snapshots, retry intents and role permissions are preserved. Cash tender/change facts are stored in receipt snapshots and audit evidence; report cash totals use the net payment amount, excluding change.
