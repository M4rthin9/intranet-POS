# Payment and PromptPay workflow

The system resolves your operating context automatically from the event assigned by Super Admin. Payment Cashier opens `/payments` directly, with no menu, dashboard or shift controls. Scan the same customer QR in the primary input. Review the customer's name, orders, item details, purchase times and exact unpaid sum. The desktop workspace places the balance beside the PromptPay QR and bank-evidence form; smaller screens stack the sections. A settled account shows confirmed payments, receipt copies and a next-customer button that clears the finished workspace and focuses the scanner. Receipt reprinting and payment history are expandable utilities below the current transaction.

Generate PromptPay: the server sums RECORDED orders, creates/reuses one awaiting-confirmation payment per account, and generates the QR locally. No manual amount is accepted. Pending settlement locks new orders. QR creation does not change account status to settled.

Verify the recipient, exact amount, transfer time and bank evidence. Enter the unique bank reference and verification notes, check the evidence checkbox, and confirm. One serializable transaction marks the payment CONFIRMED, creates exact allocations, marks included orders PAID, settles the account, snapshots the receipt and writes its audit. A retry uses the same request key; repeated confirmation returns the existing receipt.

Click **พิมพ์ QR พร้อมเพย์** beside the generated QR or in a pending payment's history row. The coordinated 80 × 120 mm card shows the exact payment amount, customer, payment number and original recipient. Every preview/print request validates the pending balance, active account, permissions and your open shift, and is audited. Printing never confirms payment. If the payment is voided or confirmed while the preview is open, another print attempt hides the stale QR and shows the server error.

Every original and COPY receipt uses an **80 mm** layout, equal 4 mm margins, 72 mm content and a height that fits its items. Use 80 mm paper at 100% scale with browser headers/footers disabled. The server audits each requested print and labels every request after the first COPY. Browser APIs cannot prove paper physically printed: `printCount` records issued print attempts. Printer failure never reverses settlement. Use a reprint rather than accepting money again.

PromptPay PHONE/TAX_ID/EWALLET settings are visible/editable only to SUPER_ADMIN. No recipient is seeded in production. The payload uses dynamic initiation, PromptPay merchant template, THB, exact integer-derived decimal amount and CRC16/CCITT-FALSE. Sources: [Bank of Thailand QR standard](https://www.bot.or.th/content/dam/bot/documents/th/our-roles/payment-systems/about-payment-systems/ThaiQRCode_Payment_Standard.pdf), [reference implementation](https://github.com/dtinth/promptpay-qr). This is manual verification, not a banking API or automated proof of transfer. Live recipient acceptance must be tested with the institution's bank before launch.
